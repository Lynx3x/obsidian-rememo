# How to Take Smart Notes

Sönke Ahrens — *How to Take Smart Notes*.

The loop: **fleeting notes** → literature notes → permanent notes. The point is not to
collect, it is to write in your own words while the thought is still warm — otherwise you
are just moving paper around.

> You have to externalise your thinking. Everything else is decoration.

## Why it sticks

- One idea per note, written as if for a stranger.
- Link it to what you already have; the links are the thinking.
- Reread the links, not the pile — structure emerges late, not early.
